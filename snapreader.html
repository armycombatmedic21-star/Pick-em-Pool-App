Instructions

Stay on this page, or reload it every 15 seconds. When a photo of a paper football pool sheet appears below, the sheet has numbered lines with a team name handwritten on each line. Read the lines in order and type them in the box in exactly this format: 1 Team, 2 Team, 3 Team … 15 Team. Write each team as it appears on the sheet, and use the list of this week's games to work out messy handwriting. Every team must come from a different game. Then tap Send. If the page says something is wrong, fix that line and tap Send again. If the photo isn’t a pool sheet or you can’t read it, tap Can’t Read This. Do not add anything else.
