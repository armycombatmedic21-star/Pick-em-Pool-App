    Preview Menu

    Simulations Preview modes for testing, one at a time. Locked with the admin code.
    Simulate live games Fills the Score Tracker with fake in-progress games so you can see how it looks on a Sunday. Real results are never touched.
    Monday night preview Every game final except one, with a pretend pool, so you can see "Tonight decides it." Nobody's real picks or scores are touched.
    Sunday night preview Every Sunday game final except the night game, with Monday night still to come and a pretend pool, so you can see the Sunday Night Football banner. Nobody's real picks or scores are touched.
    Score flash preview A sped-up afternoon on Live: a score about every 6 seconds, so you can watch the flash, the badge and the extra point, and the badge clearing when the next drive starts. Pretend scores only.
    Admin Take a player off this week's standings, or delete chat messages. Only on this phone.

    What's new

    3.7.1 Advanced leaderboard: the win % now also counts who has the ball and where, so it's sharper late in close games.
    3.7.0 Leaderboard: tap the small Basic button to switch to Advanced, which adds each player's chance to win the week. The first time, a short note explains how it works. Your phone remembers your choice. Tap it again for Basic.
    3.6.1 Live cards are tighter so team names fit on narrow Android phones: the clock column is slimmer, the football sits right against the score, and the gaps are smaller. Previews no longer mix in real results.

Tonight decides it now waits for the day of the last game, says "Already decided" when nobody can catch the leader, and both banners show team logos and the margin. Live cards flash when a team scores, with a badge (TD, FG, Safety, extra point, two-point try) until the next drive starts. The leading score is bolder and team names get more room. Online dots show up within seconds.
