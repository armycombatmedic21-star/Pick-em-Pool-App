    What's new

    2.7.1 Small fixes under the hood.
    2.6.0 Spreads no longer vanish when a game kicks off. ESPN stops sending the line once play starts, and the app was wiping its own copy — which also scrambled the sheet order. The line you picked against now stays put through the game and after it goes final.
    2.5.1 Moved Add App to Home up under the title so it stops pushing the games down the screen.
    2.5.0 Add App to Home is now a button under the week picker instead of being buried in this menu. Switching weeks now clears out the previous week's scores and leaderboard properly instead of showing them until the next refresh.
    2.4.0 The app now updates itself. It checks for a newer build when you open it and quietly reloads into it — no more stale versions stuck on a home screen. It never interrupts you mid-pick, and your sheet is untouched.
    2.3.2 Dropped the all-favorites comparison from the Score Tracker.
    2.3.1 Fixed duplicate names piling up on the leaderboard. If your phone's storage gets cleared, your old entry is now cleaned up when you post again instead of sitting there forever.
    2.3.0 Locked sheets are now protected: a short or failed schedule response can no longer drop games off a sheet you already locked, and an old saved sheet is never discarded just because the app changed. The add-to-home-screen offer no longer appears on its own — it lives in this menu.
    2.2.1 Android now goes straight to Chrome's own Install dialog instead of showing a warning-looking popup first.
    2.2.0 Cleaner first run. New phones get one popup offering to add the app to the home screen — worth doing, it stops the browser clearing your picks. You're only asked your name when it matters: locking in, or opening Live. Add to Home Screen also lives in this menu.
    2.1.0 New phones get a one-time offer to add the pool to their home screen, and there's an Add to Home Screen button in this menu any time. Worth doing — a home-screen copy keeps the browser from clearing your saved picks.
    2.0.2 Fixed locked sheets getting wiped when the live schedule loaded — picks, order and dropped games are now matched by the teams playing, not by an internal id that could change.
    2.0.1 Three tabs instead of four — Season Standings folds back into the bottom of Live, where it was. Cleared out unused code left over from the rebuild.
    2.0.0 Rebuilt around a tab bar: Picks, Sheet, Live and Standings. No more popups stacked on popups — View Sheet, the Score Tracker button and the Season Standings button are gone, replaced by tabs. Back moves between tabs instead of leaving the app.
    1.15.1 Fixed the app failing to load — a cleanup in 1.14.1 removed code the Season Standings button needed.
    1.15.0 The Score Tracker is now a full screen instead of a popup panel — no more app header peeking out behind it.
    1.14.1 Dropped the extra line under Season Standings — just the table now.
    1.14.0 Live games now show the clock in green, who has the ball, and the down and distance — and say "Halftime" instead of "Q2 0:00". Logos no longer blink on refresh, and live games refresh every 10 seconds. Added a Week # of 18 header.
    1.13.1 Tightened up the Score Tracker — about two more games fit on screen without scrolling. Nothing moved, just less empty space.
    1.13.0 The banner now says whether your picks are locked or unlocked, and there's a gold Lock In Picks button right on the main screen — no need to open the sheet first.
    1.12.1 Fixed dragging games on Android — cards no longer get dropped when the page scrolls out from under them. The banner no longer claims your order is locked when it isn't.
    1.12.0 Bigger, roomier game cards in the Score Tracker. Kickoff now reads "Today" or "Tomorrow" when it's that close, otherwise the date, with the time underneath.
    1.11.2 The Score Tracker now opens no matter what. If the leaderboard or standings ever hit trouble, only that section says so — your scores still show.
    1.11.1 Season Standings moved back below the game cards.
    1.11.0 Season Standings is now a button, sitting just under the Leaderboard instead of buried at the bottom. It shows who's leading and where you stand without opening it, and glows for your first few visits so it's easy to find.
    1.10.1 Season Standings keep themselves up to date — no need to hit Refresh. They also show instantly from the last copy on your phone instead of sitting blank while they load.
    1.10.0 Season Standings replaces the old season log: every player, every week, side by side, with a running total. Past weeks are rescored from the final scoreboard so a score can't get stuck because someone closed the app early.
    1.9.0 The app now opens on the week the league is actually on. The Score Tracker says "final" once every game of the week is played — Monday night included — and stops refreshing. Nothing gets deleted; each week keeps its own leaderboard.
    1.8.2 Fixed the Score Tracker refusing to open once an earlier week had results saved. Simulated games can no longer post a fake score to the pool. Removed games stay parked at the bottom after a spread refresh. Your score now posts on every auto-refresh, not just manual ones.
    1.8.1 Removed games drop to the bottom of the list. Restoring puts them back at their spread position.
    1.8.0 Leaderboard scores now update for everyone even while their app is closed. Remove is a small ✕ in the corner; Restore works properly again. Added this change log.
    1.7.2 Fixed the layout blowing up on Android phones with larger system font settings. Leaderboard refreshes every 5 seconds.
    1.7.1 Stopped city names breaking in half on narrow screens.
    1.7.0 Simulation now shows wins, losses, live and upcoming games. Won cards get a green border, lost cards a red X.
    1.6.2 Unlocking removes you from the leaderboard.
    1.6.0 Added the pool leaderboard.
    1.5.0 Tap a confidence number to move a game straight to that slot.
    1.4.0 Added this preview menu and the simulate-games option.
