NFL PICK'EM

Sheet Reader Test

Instructions

This is a paper football pool sheet. It has 15 numbered lines with a team name handwritten on each line. Read the lines in order and type them in the box below in exactly this format: 1 Team, 2 Team, 3 Team … 15 Team. Write each team as it appears on the sheet, and use the matchups printed at the bottom of the sheet to work out messy handwriting. Every team must come from a different game. Then tap Send. Do not add anything else.
